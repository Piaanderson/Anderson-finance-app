Turn 1 — accounts

Accounts

Three different answers to "what is the accounts page for". 1a treats it as the balance sheet — every account on one grid that adds up to net worth, detail inline. 1b treats it as a per-account workspace — thin roster, deep panel. 1c treats it as thirty days of runway — each account a card showing what is still coming at it, detail on its own page. The sidebar list stays on the budget page in all three; the house carries equity as its headline with the mortgage underneath; balances, dates and the roster are the same September 2026 scenario throughout.
